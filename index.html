Why a noodle factory

Because that's how the work runs: like a kitchen line, one order at a time.

  1. Order in

    Every idea is written down, with what "finished" will look like, before anything gets built.

  2. On the line

    Work goes out in small batches, each one tried on its own before the next one starts.

  3. At the pass

    Nothing is called done until it has been checked against the order. If it can't be shown working, it isn't finished.
